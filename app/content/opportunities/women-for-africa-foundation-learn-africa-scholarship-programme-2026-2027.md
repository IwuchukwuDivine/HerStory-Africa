## About This Opportunity

The scholarship aims to strengthen the role of African women in the development processes of their countries by providing opportunities for training, research, and leadership.

## What You Get

- Tuition fees
- Training opportunities
- Research opportunities

## Eligibility

- Woman with nationality and residence in an African country
- Enrolled at an African university or hold a university degree issued in an African country
- Meet the specific requirements for the scholarship you are applying for

## How to Apply

Interested applicants should apply online via the provided link, and are advised to visit the website for detailed information on eligibility requirements, application guidelines and scholarship benefits.
